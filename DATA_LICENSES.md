Only curated factual relationship metadata, short original summaries and citations are included. No source photographs or descriptive articles are copied. Do not infer permission to scrape, redistribute images or relicense third-party descriptions under MIT.
- **Scientific papers**: linked for provenance and future verification. No paper PDFs or experimental tables are redistributed. The Bliss 2012 article is CC BY-NC 3.0; its license does not imply unrestricted commercial redistribution. No genomic numbers were copied into the seed.

`research/sources.json` preserves source access limitations. In particular, an indexed abstract or inaccessible table is not treated as verified experimental pairwise evidence. The UI records section assignments as historical/traditional or unresolved, never modern consensus by default.
